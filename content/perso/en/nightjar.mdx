  ## Architecture

  ```
  TMDB + IMDb ratings ──nightly sync (GitHub Actions)──▶ Postgres (Neon)
                                                          ├─ ~18,000 films, French availability per platform
                                                          └─ mood axes (rules, refined at night)
  Free text ──rule-based parsing──▶ filters + deterministic score ──▶ cards with their "why"
  Gestures (seen, liked, loved, kept, not for me) ──▶ taste profile as poles
  ```

  ## Search without paid AI

  A sentence like "tense locked-room, not too gory, under 1 h 45, on my subscriptions" is split by rules: duration, years, platforms, exclusions, context, quoted titles. Ranking is a deterministic score shown on every card, with the sentence that explains it. No request goes through a model: answers in under two seconds, at zero cost.

  ## Mood, measured

  Genres are too coarse ("horror" puts a slow psychological film next to a jump-scare one). Each film gets seven continuous axes (tension, jump scares, gore, humour, pace…): a first rule-based pass at every sync, then a nightly refinement by Claude on the subscription, never during a search.

  ## Technical choices

  - **Taste as poles**: each loved film is a pole of its own, with its own era; an era factor avoids suggesting a 1950s film to someone who only likes recent ones.
  - **Diversity**: MMR selection so the five suggestions are not five copies of the same film.
  - **Sync outside Vercel**: the initial import is about 18,000 calls, it runs in GitHub Actions, with a rate limiter on a monotonic clock.
  - **Watching together**: a shared link, guests without an account who vote, a common ranking.
  - **Machine bridges** by token: the Memogram journal sends the films you talk about, Magpie the films spotted in saved videos; Nightjar stays the single source of truth for a film's state.
  - **Layers**: short Server Actions and routes → services → repositories (Drizzle). Vitest tests on PGlite, no network; ten written architecture decisions.

# Nightjar

The right film in under a minute: say what you feel like watching the way you would on the couch, Nightjar answers with a poster, its reason, and where to watch it tonight.

## Context

Personal project started on 30/10/2026, live since 02/11. Born from an evening spent looking for a film longer than watching it.

## Features

**Choosing**:
- "Tonight": one main film and four others, tuned by mood, length, who you are with, and only on your subscriptions
- Free-text mood, with the understood words highlighted and editable
- "Like…": neighbours of one or more starting films
- Trailer, rating, length and platforms on every card

**Learning taste**:
- Seen, liked, loved, keep, not for me, more or less like this
- Quick calibration on posters at first launch
- A bank of kept films, with those just arrived on your platforms

**Together**:
- A watch party shared by link, guests without an account, voting and a common ranking
