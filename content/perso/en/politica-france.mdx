  ## Principle

  The priority was set from day one: accuracy > completeness > freshness, and no invented data. Every displayed value comes from a raw snapshot kept with its provenance; the whole database can be re-derived from those snapshots.

  ```
  Official sources ─► Connectors ─► Raw snapshot + provenance (content_hash)
                                          │
                                          ▼
                      Identities + bitemporal versioned facts ─► Services ─► Pages
  ```

  ## Data model

  - **Identity + fact satellites** versioned bitemporally (what was true, and when we learned it)
  - Each value typed `FAIT_SOURCE` (taken from a source) or `ANALYSE_DERIVEE` (calculation documented in the methodology)
  - Idempotent ingestion: a replayed job creates no duplicates

  ## Connectors

  French National Assembly (MPs, votes, amendments), Senate, government composition (DILA), 2024 legislative elections (Ministry of the Interior), European Parliament, Wikidata (descriptions, left-right placement) and Wikipedia (party summaries), the last two flagged as third-party sources. The amendments connector filters the archive by file name so that only the targeted bill is unpacked.

  ## Public mode

  - Rate limiting, CSP and HSTS headers
  - Legal notice, GDPR page, data protection impact assessment (DPIA), per-entity correction requests
  - Robots and sitemap depending on the mode (private or public)

  ## Quality

  - Route (≤ 20 lines) → service → repository layers; the database is imported only in repositories
  - Network-free tests (a real-network health signal runs separately, non-blocking)
  - Fixed headcounts checked (577 MPs, 348 senators), secret scan before commit
  - Decisions recorded as ADRs; light and dark palettes checked for AA contrast

# Politica France

Who sits where, who voted for what: French political life, sourced line by line.

## Context

Personal project started in June 2026 and published online. Phases 0 to 6 and seven follow-up iterations are delivered. The project is on pause, waiting for feedback from its first testers.

## Features

**People and organisations**:
- French MPs, senators, government members and MEPs, with search on every keystroke
- Profiles with mandates and attendance rate
- Parties and groups: description, neutral summary, link to the official programme, left-right placement

**Votes and bills**:
- Votes with For / Against / Abstain results, individual votes collapsed
- Amendments: lifecycle from filing to outcome, text and explanatory statement, search by author, group or bill

**Territory and relationships**:
- Constituency map and 2024 legislative election results
- Relationship graph between political actors

**Transparency**:
- Methodology and sources page, CSV export with provenance, correction requests
- Light or dark theme, automatic or manual
