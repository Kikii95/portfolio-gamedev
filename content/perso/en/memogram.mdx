  ## Architecture

  Two halves: an always-reachable server, and a PC that does the heavy lifting when it is on.

  ```
  Telegram (voice/text) ──webhook──▶ Next.js on Vercel + Postgres (Neon)
                                      ├─ immediate cloud transcription (Mistral Voxtral)
                                      ├─ summaries (Mistral): evening cron, Monday, 1st of month
                                      └─ PC work queue
                                                   ▲
  PC on ── Python worker (every 15 min) ───────────┘
           ├─ re-transcribes voice notes (faster-whisper large-v3, GPU)
           ├─ writes the summaries the cloud could not (claude -p)
           ├─ archives voice notes locally
           └─ mirrors the journal into the Obsidian vault
  ```

  ## Nothing depends on credit

  Every cloud pass returns a typed result; a failure (missing key, exhausted credit, outage) moves the work to the PC queue instead of losing it. The bot says the transcription will come the next time the PC is on.

  ## Technical choices

  - **Two transcriptions**: Voxtral, guided by a vocabulary of proper names, is the reference; Whisper on the PC is only used when the cloud failed, and never overwrites a manual correction.
  - **No audio stored server-side**: the Telegram file id is kept, the archive lives on the PC.
  - **Explicit states**: an entry goes `pending_cloud` → `pending_local` → `done`; any edit marks the summaries covering its date as stale, regenerated by the next cron.
  - **A single cron** (Vercel Hobby compatible) that catches up missed days, regenerates from day up to year, and sends the evening draft.
  - **Memory-frugal worker**: it waits for enough free RAM, volunteers as the first target when memory runs out, and retries on the next pass without loss.
  - **No "logical" LLM correction** of the text: in a journal, inventing is worse than misspelling.
  - **Layers**: short routes → services → repositories (Drizzle). Vitest tests on PGlite, no network; pytest on the worker side.

  ## Bridges to other apps

  A structured extraction spots what is expressed as an intention (task, reminder, idea) and announces it before handing it to the JARVIS assistant, with 10 minutes to cancel. Nights, job applications and expenses mentioned in a note go to the dedicated apps only when backed by a quote from the note.

# Memogram

A journal you keep without opening an app: you tell your day as it goes to a Telegram bot, which transcribes, files and summarises it.

## Context

Personal project started on 13/10/2026, at version 0.8.0 since 17/10. Deliberately kept apart from JARVIS: Memogram stays a journal and only forwards what looks like a request.

## Features

**Telling**:
- Notes by voice, text or captioned photo
- Correct by replying to the note, or add to it with `+`
- The day ends at 7 am; an account of another day ("my Thursday") is filed under the right day

**Reading back**:
- A summary every evening, then every week, month and year
- Commands `/aujourdhui`, `/hier`, `/semaine`, `/mois`, `/cherche`, `/qui`
- Search by meaning, without the exact word
- "One year ago" in the evening message
- Reading PWA opened by a single-use link sent by the bot: day, week, month, people, search always visible

**Remembering**:
- Daily memory exercise: recall a past note from memory, spaced reviews
- Before a meeting: what the journal knows about the person
