  ## Principle

  A 100% deterministic engine: no AI, every target and every recommendation comes from a published formula, shown expanded on screen. The engines are pure modules, with no database, tested against vectors, with their sources cited in the code.

  ```
  Screens (workout, meals, review) ─► Services ─► Repositories ─► PostgreSQL
                                        │
                                        ├─► lib/nutrition (targets, trend, expenditure, review)
                                        └─► lib/training  (programs, progression, e1RM, plateau)
  ```

  ## Nutrition engine

  - Basal metabolism: Mifflin-St Jeor, Katch-McArdle, Cunningham
  - Weight trend: exponential moving average (α = 0.1), missing days interpolated
  - Measured expenditure: logged intake minus change in stores (7,700 kcal/kg), least-squares line over 2 to 8 weeks; without enough data the app refuses to measure and says why
  - Sunday review: new target, change capped at ±200 kcal per review
  - Simulator of 8 weeks of noisy fictional life: expenditure recovered within ±50 kcal (median 21 kcal over 200 draws)

  ## Training engine

  - 44 exercises in French, 3 programs (full body 3 d, upper/lower 4 d, PPL 6 d)
  - Double progression, Epley e1RM, records
  - Plateau detected after 3 sessions without progress → lighter session; deload week on signal

  ## Offline

  The workout log works in airplane mode: every action goes into a local queue replayed in order, without duplicates (IDs generated by the phone). The service worker caches static assets only, never health data.

  ## Data and compliance

  - Single account created through a first-login link, passkeys (WebAuthn), no public sign-up
  - Database hosted in the EU; GDPR export and erasure covering every table
  - Foods: embedded CIQUAL table + Open Food Facts called server-side with a 7-day cache; values frozen at entry time
  - Additive migrations applied by the production build; a destructive migration fails both tests and build

# HaltèrEgo

A strength-training and nutrition coach that shows its calculations instead of asking to be trusted.

## Context

Personal project started on 20/10/2026, in development. A private app, designed for a single user. Version 0.4.0 covers the first five phases of the plan: foundation, "the instrument" art direction, nutrition engine, workouts, food log and weekly review. The last three are awaiting validation in real conditions.

## Features

**Nutrition**:
- Calorie and macro targets computed during onboarding, each number with its formula
- Food log: accent-tolerant search, favourites, custom foods, "same as yesterday"
- Morning weigh-in and remaining kcal for the day

**Workouts**:
- Ready-made programs, thumb-friendly log with rest timer and screen kept awake
- Suggested progression with the calculation expanded, history and records
- Works without a network at the gym

**Weekly loop**:
- Sunday review: trend curve, measured expenditure, new target explained in plain words
- Plateau detection and deload week
