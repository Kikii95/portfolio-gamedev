  ## Pitch

  Cali, a humanoid creature from the **Primoris** people, crash-lands on the planet **Hestia** after an engine failure. She wakes up with no memory, taken in by the **Persephins**. With the help of **Iris**, a brave young Persephin, she discovers the planet is being torn apart by the **Harmathys corruption** — a defense mechanism triggered by the Primoris arrival, turning the once-peaceful inhabitants into aggressive creatures.

  ## Genre & gameplay loop

  Hybrid **village management** + **action/exploration expeditions**. The player alternates between two phases:

  ```
  ┌──────────────────┐         ┌──────────────────┐
  │  VILLAGE PHASE   │ ◄─────► │ EXPEDITION PHASE │
  │ (iso management) │ cycles  │   (action WASD)  │
  ├──────────────────┤         ├──────────────────┤
  │ • Drag & drop    │         │ • Combat         │
  │ • Buildings      │         │ • Puzzles        │
  │ • Population     │         │ • Resource farm  │
  │ • Heroes         │         │ • Bosses         │
  └──────────────────┘         └──────────────────┘
  ```

  Each expedition consumes a village **cycle** (1 ration per villager). The village expands from 3×3 → 10×10 parcels of 7×7 tiles thanks to map fragments brought back from expeditions.

  ## Universe — 6 peoples

  | People | Region | Traits |
  |--------|--------|--------|
  | Persephins | Central plains | Nomadic traders, social |
  | Yōtl | Sand dunes | Warriors, honor code |
  | Arynes | Crimson forests | Artists, festive |
  | Kaprass | Mountains | Astrologers, secluded |
  | Khétus | Coasts | Navigators, fishermen |
  | Primoris | Space | Colonizers, advanced tech |

  Five peoples = five playable biomes with their own resources, enemies and atmosphere. The Primoris are the unwitting cause of the corruption.

  ## Village mechanics

  Five core buildings:

  - **Tree of Life** — Central building, caps the level of every other building
  - **Housing** — Raises villager capacity
  - **Hospital** — Purifies corrupted villagers + heals heroes
  - **Forge** — Crafts equipment (T1-T4) + rerolls stats via gems
  - **Barracks** — Sends out **excursions** (1-3 heroes, autonomous)

  ## Expedition mechanics

  - **5 biomes × 4 zones** + **Endless** mode unlocked after zone 16
  - **5 hero classes**: DPS, Tank, Healer, Gatherer, Treasure Hunter
  - **T1-T5 tiers** on resources, gear and traits
  - **6 gem colors** for reroll (colorless = base material, colored = lock a trait)
  - **5 puzzle types**: Piano, Pressure Plate, Crate-Plate, Tangram, Memory
  - Rich stat system: armor, strength, speed, fortune, luck, crit, combo, chain, range…

# Ruins Of Hestia

Team game built for the Gaming Campus Awards 25-26. My role: dev on the custom C++/Vulkan engine.

## Context

Grand Projet Commun Bachelor 3A — 17 people across five departments (Tech, Art, Business, TV, Sound). Five-month production from January to late April 2026.

## Concept

Hybrid **management** + **action/exploration**. You manage a Persephin village from an isometric drag & drop view, and run expeditions in a fixed isometric WASD view to gather resources, fight corruption and push Cali's story forward.

Five playable biomes (one per people), 5 hero classes, T1-T5 tier system on everything (resources, gear, traits), reroll via colored gems, embedded puzzles (Piano, Plate, Crate, Tangram, Memory), endless mode unlocked after zone 16.

## Stack & engine

C++20, Vulkan 1.3, CMake + vcpkg. Custom engine: 5 libs (Framework, Render, Audio, Assets, Engine ECS) + scenes. Graphics pipeline with GLSL/SPIR-V shaders, compute pipeline for GPU particles. Tile-based procedural generation for expedition maps.
