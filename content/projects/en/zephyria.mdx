# Zephyria

Game Design Document for an aerial adventure-exploration game: in a world drowned under an ocean of toxic clouds, the
Veil, humanity survives on floating islands that the player travels by flying on a Souffleur.

## Context

Personal project from April 2026, at the pre-production stage: the design document is written, the pitch deck, the
prototype and the visuals are planned. It follows an earlier GDD, "Éolia", written the same morning, and reuses its market study.

## What I did

The whole GDD (gameplay, world, level design, art direction, sound, tech, production), a market study and a brief on
the legal framework, and the document's build chain.
