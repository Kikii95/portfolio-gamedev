# C++ exercises and breakout

The C++ exercises and labs of the 2024-2025 year at Gaming Campus, from the first encapsulation day to an
entity-component architecture.

## Context

A series of small repositories, one per exercise or lab, over six months. The only team project is the SFML breakout
from November 2024.

## What I did

C++ OOP basics, a state machine, scenes on the LightEngine engine, threads, a TCP client and server, an entity-component
architecture, and my version of the breakout game.
