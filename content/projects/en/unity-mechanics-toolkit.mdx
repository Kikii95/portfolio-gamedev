# Unity Mechanics Toolkit

Unity toolkit gathering the classic mechanics of a first-person horror game, ready to drop into a scene.

## Context

Personal project started in November 2025.

## What I did

About forty C# scripts organised by module (movement, horror, camera, AI, interaction, combat, audio), demo scene
generators in the editor, the documentation and the tutorials.
