  - **Dungeons**: generated from room templates, in a dedicated dimension, with score, timer and rank-based loot.
  - **Creatures**: custom mobs and bosses with a boss bar.

# Overpower

Minecraft mod adding a full action-RPG layer: classes, skills, spells, equipment and dungeons.

## Context

Personal project from February 2026, the first mod of a workspace dedicated to Minecraft modding (Fabric), with its
build scripts.

## What I did

The mod's game design, then its implementation in Java: class and resource system, skill trees, spells and effects,
equipment, creatures, and dungeon generation with its scoring logic.
