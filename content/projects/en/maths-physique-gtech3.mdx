# Engine maths and physics

3rd-year maths and physics work (Gaming Campus, 2025-2026), all rendered in the console.

## Context

The engine maths course covered 3D-to-2D rotations and projections, with an ASCII donut as the common thread. The
team exam asked to make that donut float and spin. The advanced physics module started with a week of integration
exercises.

## What I did

The course's ASCII renderer (meshes, projection, settings), the donut's orbit animation for the exam, and the Euler
integration exercises.
