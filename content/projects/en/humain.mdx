# Human

Introspective mobile app to bring people closer through questions that matter. No winner, no score - just listening.

## Context

Idea and first prototype in August 2024. Project restarted in 2026, full release planned for late 2026. Designed and built solo. Personal IP.

## What it does

**11 themes** - Each round digs into one topic (self, others, time passing, fear…). 5 question pairs per theme.

**Mirror mechanic** - For each subject, two complementary questions: one that pushes you to share what hurts, one that pushes you to acknowledge what helps. You choose which side to flip.

**"Toward others" variant** - Instead of answering for yourself, you guess what the other person would say.

**Filterable catalog** - All questions browsable outside a game, by theme.

## Distribution

Closed beta planned for late May 2026. EAS build for iOS and Android. Private repo.
